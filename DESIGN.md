# Intelligence Atlas visual system

Finale supersedes the Nightfall visual treatment. The product combines an expressive editorial landing with precise evidence workbenches and an interactive dependency instrument. Midnight ink, porcelain, mint and amber provide distinct contexts for possibility, evidence and uncertainty. Georgia is a system display face; system sans/Devanagari fallbacks retain readable Hindi. No font downloads or new dependencies.

Reusable components: IntelligenceMap (deterministic, bounded display, selection/hover, pan, zoom and measured fit), AssessmentLedger (actual condition outcomes), ReadinessJourney (profile/process steps), CapturedReader (searchable captured evidence). The map displays at most six priority actions and six opportunities; the full checklist remains available. Mobile prioritizes the checklist and evidence instead of compressed graph nodes.

Public landing connections are explicitly illustrative. Public coverage comes from actual publication counts. Populated test screenshots are explicitly fictional contracts. Never fill empty states with invented policy or personalized results. Source wording remains original; EN/HI static controls and deterministic local explanations do not imply validated legal translation.

See docs/finale-visual-direction.md for principles and docs/finale-final-report.md for inspected screenshots, validation and limitations. Existing styles retain base form/state primitives; finale.css defines the new compositions and overrides. Further stylesheet consolidation is maintenance work, not a claim of finished design certification.
